A dish that speaks of the sea and the soil in equal measure. The bass is seared to crisp-skinned perfection, served over a silken beurre blanc that carries the bright acidity of citrus and the subtle bite of shallots. Simple. Precise. Unforgettable.

Our Ingredients

Premium Beef

Sourced from pasture-raised cattle, dry-aged for a minimum of 28 days to concentrate flavor and tenderness. We work exclusively with farms that prioritize animal welfare and sustainable practices. The result is beef that tastes of the land it came from — deep, rich, and unmistakably honest.

Fresh Seafood

Delivered daily from coastal waters, never frozen. Our fish and shellfish are hand-selected for their freshness and seasonality. We follow the tides, not the calendar — if it's not at its peak, it does not appear on our menu. Simple as that.

Farm Fresh

Produce that travels from soil to plate in hours, not days. We partner with local growers who cultivate heirloom varieties and harvest at the moment of ripeness. The flavor is brighter, the texture crisper, and the connection to the land undeniable.

Preparation Process

1

Ingredients

Each fillet is chosen for its firmness, brightness, and sheen. Herbs come from our rooftop garden, picked just hours before service.

2

Preparation

The fish is dried thoroughly and scored lightly for even cooking. Shallots are minced fine, herbs washed and spun dry. Every element prepped to precision.

3

Cooking

A hot pan, skin-side down, and then we let it be. The skin crisps without interference. For the sauce, shallots are sweated, deglazed, and mounted with cold butter for a silky emulsion.

4

Plating

A pool of sauce, the bass centered, herbs scattered. A final arc of herb oil. Plated and sent, within seconds of finishing.

Chef’s Note

“One cannot think well, love well, sleep well, if one has not dined well.” --Marco Rossi

Our kitchen runs on a simple belief: the best dish is the one that lets its ingredients speak for themselves. We do not chase trends or complicate the plate with unnecessary flourishes. Instead, we focus on technique that serves the ingredient, not the other way around. Every sauce is made from scratch, every herb picked at its peak, every plate finished with the same care we would demand for ourselves. This is not just how we cook, it is who we are.

From the first welcome to the last bite, every detail is designed to create a
memorable dining experience for you and your loved ones.
