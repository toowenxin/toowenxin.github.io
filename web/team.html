Meet Our Team

Behind every plate is a person. Behind every person is a story. We are a small team by choice, close-knit, cross-trained, and driven by the same quiet obsession: to make every dish worthy of your time.

The Team

Executive Chef

Marco Rossi

Marco's journey began not in a culinary school, but in his grandmother's kitchen in Tuscany, where he learned that the best recipes are passed down through hands, not written in books. After training in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, including three years at a three-star establishment in France, he brought his philosophy of ingredient-led cooking to this restaurant. He believes that technique serves the ingredient, not the other way around. Off the clock, Marco can be found at the local fish market before dawn, selecting each piece of seafood by hand. He does not chase trends. He chases flavor.

Specialties

Italian Regional Cuisine · Seared Fish & Shellfish · Sauce Emulsions · Seasonal Pasta

Chef

Yuki Tanaka

Yuki grew up in Kyoto, where she trained in the precise art of wagashi, traditional Japanese confections, before moving to Paris to study patisserie. Her work bridges two worlds: the delicate restraint of Japanese aesthetics and the rich indulgence of French pastry. She sources local fruits and herbs for her desserts, but her real signature is texture, crisp, creamy, airy, and dense all within a single spoonful. She is the first to arrive in the kitchen and the last to leave, not because she has to, but because she cannot stop until every plate is worthy of a second look.

Specialties

French-Japanese Fusion Pastry · Chocolate & Caramel Work · Fruit-Based Desserts · Sugar Art
