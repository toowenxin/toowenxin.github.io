Reception & Bar

Begin your dining experience in style.

A Refined Meal
Fine Dining Experience

Fine dining is not about formality. It is about intention. Every element, the lighting, the pacing, the weight of the silverware, the temperature of the plate, is considered not for show, but for your comfort. We do not rush. We do not distract. We simply create the conditions for you to be fully present, with the food, with the company, with the moment. This is not dining as performance. It is dining as it should be: thoughtful, unhurried, and quietly memorable.

A classic reimagined, an original definition
Signature Cocktails

Original creations born from our bartender's instinct and the season's best. Each signature is crafted in-house, not for novelty, but for balance. We start with a classic structure, then introduce unexpected local accents: a house-infused herb, a hand-cut ice block, a garnish that tells a story. These are drinks you will not find elsewhere. Not because they are secret, but because they are shaped by this moment, this kitchen, this bar.

Classic Martini

The benchmark of a bartender's precision. Gin or vodka, dry vermouth, a whisper of orange bitters. Stirred, never shaken — unless you insist. Served ice-cold, with a twist or an olive.

Negroni

Equal parts gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth — a formula that has survived a century for good reason. We build it over a single large ice cube, stirring slowly until the dilution is just right. A classic that needs no improvement, only respect.

Old Fashioned

Bourbon or rye, a sugar cube, Angostura bitters. No muddled fruit, no sparkling water, just whiskey, bitters, and a twist of orange.

Mocktails

Zero proof. Full character. For those who prefer flavor without alcohol, we offer drinks that are anything but an afterthought. Fresh-pressed juices, house-made syrups, herbs from our garden, balanced, complex, and worthy of the same glassware.
