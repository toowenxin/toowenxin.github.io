Frequently Asked Questions
FAQS
Do You Accept Reservations? ^

Yes, we accept table reservations.

You can book your table through our website or contact us directly to secure your preferred time.

What are your operating hours? ^

We are open daily from 12:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Please note that our last order is at 9:30 PM.

Do you offer delivery or takeaway services? ^

Yes, we offer both options to enjoy our food at home.

For take away, you can order at the counterfor pickup. You can also visit our website for direct delivery.

Is there a dress code at your restaurant? ^

We kindly request smart casual attire. Jackets are not required, but we appreciate a polished presentation,

as a mark of respect for the dining experience and your fellow guests.

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options? ^

Yes, we proudly offer a curated selection of gourmet vegetarian and vegan options.

Each dish is thoughtfully prepared with the same passion and culinary mastery as our signature menu.
